## Introduction — Weapons of Gods, Instincts of Apes

This is the foundation. We live in an era in which we possess the technology of gods — the atom, artificial intelligence, genetic engineering — but our brains still run on old software, the kind we inherited from our ancient ancestors. Tribal instincts, survival fears, and a relentless pursuit of status.

This document explains how, from that same basic "ape" operating system, through layers of ego, group, economy, and technology — we built a global system so complex that the individual simply becomes lost within it.

Understanding this system is the key to knowing how to change it.

## 1. The Primary Engine: The Ego

Everything begins with the ego. The ego is not inherently evil; it is simply the individual's basic operating system. It is composed of several deep layers that operate ceaselessly:

- **The biological-cellular layer:** Neurons fire, hormones such as dopamine, cortisol, and oxytocin are released. The body demands survival: food, sex, physical safety. This is the machine.
- **The survival-emotional layer:**
  - **Fear:** The most ancient engine. Fear of death, fear of the unknown, fear of rejection. Fear activates the "fight or flight" system and causes us to withdraw into ourselves or to attack.
  - **Love/belonging:** The engine that balances fear. The need to be part of something greater than ourselves, to be loved, to be protected.
  - **Drive:** A basic impulse toward action — for good (cooperation, protecting the weak) or for ill (aggression, violence), depending on context and circumstances.
- **The recognition and status layer (the social ego):** The deepest need — to be seen. The need for self-worth set against external value. The ego's perpetual questions are: "Where am I on the ladder?", "Who is above me?" and "Who is below me?" Status is the most ancient psychological currency, and it remains the hidden driver of the vast majority of human actions.

The ego, when entirely alone, is chaos seeking meaning.

## 2. The Encounter: How the Ego Generates Micro-Culture

The moment two egos (or more) meet, friction is created. This friction is the raw material from which micro-culture is born — the operating system of the small group, such as a family, a clique, or a work team.

- **Unwritten rules:** What is funny and what is offensive? When does one remain silent? Who is allowed to speak first?
- **Hidden hierarchy:** Even without words, the group scans itself and builds status rankings. Who is the natural leader? Who is the clown? Who is the wise one? Who is the scapegoat who is always picked on?
- **Private language:** Inside jokes, abbreviations, code names. This language is the glue that turns the group into a single cohesive unit.
- **Accelerated status dynamics:** Within the small group, the ego seeks validation in the most overt and immediate way. We adjust our behavior in order to be accepted, and we put on "masks" in accordance with what is required in each and every group.

The small group is a laboratory. In it, we test who we can be, and receive for it an immediate and merciless grade from the tribe.

## 3. The First Quantum Leap: From Micro-Culture to Ideology

What happens when the group grows too large? When I can no longer know everyone personally?

The micro-culture transforms into a social operating system (OS), and its binding glue is ideology or religion.

- **The glue:** Instead of loving the specific person standing in front of me, I learn to love an abstract idea, a nation, a god, or a flag. The emotion is directed toward a symbol instead of toward a human being.
- **The paper:** These shared ideas, shared by millions, become actual institutions:
  - **Politics:** It is no longer a discussion about "where shall we go to eat?" but a struggle over "who is authorized to deploy military force?" The leader's hungry ego is still there, but it is now wrapped in bombastic words such as "the good of the people" or "national security."
  - **Law:** My personal fear of the other is replaced by a social contract. Personal revenge (an ape instinct) is replaced by cold and calculated order. The system quietly promises me: "Be calm, I will avenge you in a legal and orderly manner."
  - **Economy and money:** Modern money is no longer gold or barter. It is a religion of trust. We all agree to believe that numbers on a computer screen are worth something, simply because we trust the institutions and the technology that backs this delusional agreement.

Ideology is a social technology that allows our ape brain to function within a group of millions. It strips us of personal trust and converts it into systemic and blind trust.

## 4. The Modern Leviathan: The Loss of the Individual Within the Mass

Here lies the crux. In the transition from the small group to the state, something astonishing and terrifying happens simultaneously: the personal ego is simply swallowed.

- **Altruism becomes tax:** My giving to the weak is no longer done face to face. It becomes a percentage of my salary, taken automatically and distributed by a vast, cold, and alienated mechanism that I neither know nor control. The humanity is entirely erased from the act of giving.
- **Fear becomes national security:** My existential fear of the violent neighbor is replaced by an abstract and manufactured fear of "the axis of evil" or "the immigration crisis." The system colors and channels my natural fear in order to justify its own existence — army, police, walls.
- **Politics, money, and technology — a closed loop:**
  - Money feeds technology (research, development, internet).
  - Technology shapes ideology (the algorithms on social networks determine what we see, what is correct, and what "truth" even means).
  - Ideology returns, half a turn back, and shapes the education I received in school and you received at home, in order to shape my ego so that it fits like a glove to the system.
- **The lost individual:** At the end of this engineered process, a "Leviathan" is created — a super-system that predicts your next step (by means of algorithms and big data) before you yourself have thought of it. It gives you easy dopamine so that you continue to function, directs you to a 9-to-5 job, and defines for you the clear boundaries of identity: "You are Israeli," "You are American." It will never, ever, allow you to think of yourself easily as a "human being."

The system we created from our ego gradually became a well-oiled machine that itself produces egos fitted to it and to its needs. This is the perfect loop that keeps us stuck in our current state.

## 5. Why Are We Still Stuck?

This global system may work, but it works against humanity's next quantum leap.

To advance to the next stage requires from us an entirely new operating system (OS 2.0):

- **Planetary identity:** Instead of defining myself as "I am from Israel, the USA, China," I must learn to feel and define myself as "a human being from planet Earth, Middle East district."
- **Genuine global cooperation:** Planetary resource management, joint defense against asteroids, and interstellar projects — all of these require a level of global trust that simply does not exist at present.
- **The obstacles along the way:**
  - **The ideological loop:** The system has shattered our ego into tribal fragments — nations, religions, political camps. It causes us to fight one another over resources and status, instead of cooperating toward a supreme goal.
  - **The human difficulty:** For the average person, who is on autopilot most of the time, thinking about extinction or about life on Mars is a surefire recipe for existential anxiety. Therefore, they prefer easy and regular dopamine. They cannot bear it psychologically.
  - **Time is pressing:** While we continue to bicker like apes over who is the leader, we are one spontaneous extinction event away — an asteroid, an artificial intelligence that has escaped control, catastrophic warming. Earth's natural defense system is a miracle, but a miracle is not insurance.

The Leviathan we built to protect us and help us survive has gradually become the main reason we might go extinct.

## Summary

The human species is an astonishing phenomenon. We took an ape with survival instincts, fear, an unending appetite for status, and a basic need for love — and built around it systems of immense proportions: language, culture, money, law, the internet.

These systems, originally created to protect us, have over time united into a single global Leviathan whose only true interest is self-preservation.

This Leviathan replicates and perpetuates itself through education, controls us through fear and status, and distracts us with easy and available dopamine.

This is the precise mechanism that keeps us stuck, quarreling among ourselves while the cosmic clock continues to tick.

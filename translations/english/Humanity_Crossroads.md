# Humanity at a Crossroads

## Introduction: The Step We Must Take

We live in an era in which humanity explores space and faces immense challenges. As long as we remain stuck at our current level, we are in severe existential danger.

If we do not change the paradigms and methodologies by means of which the world, the public, and all populations operate, we will remain below step 1 on Kardashev scale. Worse still, under one set of circumstances or another, we may annihilate ourselves. This is a real threat.

Only when we change our ways of thinking and acting — not at the individual level, but at the collective level — only then will we be ripe to climb this step. There are clear conditions that are not yet fully known to us, but we are working to decipher them. There are also obstacles that must be closed: for example, if we do not gain control over artificial intelligence, it may destroy us in the end, and there are additional threats requiring immediate solutions.

Only after we carry out a true and comprehensive change will we be able to ensure our survival and progress.

## Shift in Consciousness: From National Identity to Global Identity

As of today, human beings define themselves on the basis of their country, city, or district. "Where are you from?" is a simple and ordinary question. Yet we all share a single planet. At present, it is superfluous for a person to say "I am from Earth" — it is self-evident.

In order to cross the step to 1, all of humanity will have to become accustomed to the idea, and subsequently also to the practical perception, that in the future we will know each other not merely as "Michael from Los Angeles, United States," but as "Michael from planet Earth, America district, the city of Los Angeles."

This thought is revolutionary and difficult to digest for the majority of the public. Precisely because of this, most of humanity is not yet ready for this quantum leap; the transition is too rapid. We need to gradually ripen the public, politics, nations, paradigms, and methodologies, in order to enable the leap — not only to step 1, but to the infinity of what has until now been considered science fiction.

## The Human Dilemma: "Weapons of Gods, Instincts of Apes"

One can honestly describe the state of humanity, as of the year 2026, in a few harsh but precise words: we possess "weapons of gods" — nuclear weapons, artificial intelligence, genetic engineering — yet we operate with "instincts of apes."

When I say "instincts of apes," I am referring to the average behavior of the modern human. The average person operates on "autopilot" roughly 90% of the time. This behavior, which is not always bad or harmful, still demonstrates our evolution. We inherited barbaric, egotistic, and at times irrational behavior, similar to apes. This manifests especially in extreme situations, when the masks come off — for instance, under the influence of alcohol or drugs, and the person becomes violent or harmful.

In essence, every average person possesses this tendency at one level or another — whether in extreme violence or simply in indifference — as long as they are unaware of their internal problems and act out of general lack of awareness.

## The Human System: From Individual to Global "Leviathan"

The human species can be described as a single complete system, composed of numerous sub-systems, all of which influence one another.

As we ascend the levels of scale, the systems become more complex. In the modern world, everything is driven primarily by money, technology, and information.

At the national and global level, the micro-culture becomes a "social operating system." Ideology and religion serve as the glue that unites millions of people who do not know each other personally. Instead of loving a close friend, we learn to "love" the state or the idea.

Modern institutions at this level:

- **Organized politics:** Control over resources, setting laws, and use of force (police, army). The driver is still the status and ego of leaders, wrapped in "the common good."
- **The legal system:** Designed to restrain the individual's fear of the other and to replace personal revenge with cold and calculated order.
- **The global economy:** Modern money is a "religion of trust." We believe in numbers on a screen, because technology and information assure that the system is stable.

## The Conclusion: Loss and Hope

The transition to the modern system entails the loss of the individual within the mass: altruism becomes tax (national insurance), and fear becomes national security. The modern system uses algorithms and information to predict the next step of our ego.

Ultimately, we receive a giant "Leviathan" of systems: money feeds technology, technology shapes ideology, and ideology shapes the education of the child so that their ego fits exactly into the larger system.

In order to advance to step 1, we must recognize our systemic and human nature. The change must begin at the collective level: to replace the "ape instincts" driven by ego and fear with genuine global awareness driven by love and a shared desire to survive and thrive as a single entity.

This is the existential challenge of the 21st century. Will we manage to mature enough to save ourselves, or will we annihilate ourselves long before we can extend our hand toward infinity? The answer lies in our hands, through a true and comprehensive change in our ways of thinking and acting.
